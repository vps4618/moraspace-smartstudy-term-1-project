4. Find Hotspot SSID
    Open your phone's settings and navigate to Mobile Hotspot (Android) or Personal Hotspot (iOS). 
    Note down the Network Name (SSID) and the Password. 
    You can temporarily rename the SSID to something simple without spaces (like SmartStudyNet) to prevent typos in the ESP32 code.

5. Connect Laptop and Find IPv4
    Connect Laptop and Find IPv4:Windows.Connect your laptop to this mobile hotspot. 
    Open the Command Prompt and type ipconfig.
     Scroll to the section labeled Wireless LAN adapter Wi-Fi and note the IPv4 Address (it will likely start with 192.168. or 172.20.).

# important
To verify this setup is working before the ESP32 is even connected, ensure your FastAPI app is running on 0.0.0.0.
 Then, open a web browser on your phone (which is broadcasting the hotspot) and type http://YOUR_IPV4:8000/api/study-areas.
